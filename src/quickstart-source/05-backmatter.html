Combat

Setting Up Turn Order

Roll Sharp vs Entity’s Ranged Defence:

  • On a standard success, either place your token anywhere on the grid or act before the Entity.
  • On a superior success, do both.
  • On a crit success, do both and get an extra manoeuvre on your first turn.

On Each Turn, pick one:

1 attack & 1 manoeuvre

2 manoeuvres

When a Hunter turn ends, the Entity gets an Interrupt if the GM so decides and pays the cost.

Hunter Manoeuvres

  • Focus: Gain Focus, to a maximum of 1. You may spend Focus to gain advantage on your next roll.
  • Guard: Restore 2 Resolve, or attempt to revive a dying Hunter in your area.
  • Move: Travel between two adjacent (facing sides only; not diagonal) grid areas.
  • Reload: Regain 1 Capacity on a Weapon you are holding, up to its maximum.
  • Take Cover: Claim a terrain tag – roll Strong for Elevated or Quick for Sheltered. On a failure either:
  • Suffer 1 Resolve damage as you over-exert yourself; or
  • Place 1 Threat on your area as you draw attention to yourself; or
  • Return the terrain tag to the pool.
  • Use: Activate an ability, item or power as defined in its description.

Support Area and Support Manoeuvres

When you start your turn in Support, restore Capacity to max and restore 2 Resolve. You also have access to these support manoevures:

  • Recover : Roll Hard. On any success, restore 4 Resolve or 2 Wounds (your choice). On a failure, restore 2 Resolve.
  • Heal: Roll Wise. On a success, restore 2 Wounds to another Hunter in Support or restore 1 Wound to yourself. On a failure, restore 1 Wound to another Hunter in Support.

Terrain Types

  • Elevated . Roll Strong to claim. All attacks are rolled with advantage.
  • Sheltered . Roll Quick to claim. When the Entity inflicts damage on you, take 1 less damage.

Entity Standard Manoeuvres

  • Prowl. Target one area and one adjacent area. All Hunters in the first area test Quick. On a failure, they are Repositioned to the adjacent area.
  • Shrug Off. Restore 3 Resolve.
  • Turn Around. All Hunters in Close are Repositioned clockwise or counter-clockwise – choose one and apply it to all targets.

Stats

  • Strong: destructive, athletic or vicious.
  • Hard: tough, resistant, grim or intimidating.
  • Quick: fast, decisive, sneaky or deceitful.
  • Sharp: keen-eyed, aware and intelligent.
  • Wise: intuitive, introspective or mystical.

Health: Resolve and Wounds

Resolve is a measure of stamina, determination and physical fitness. Narrate Resolve damage as the target dodging, blocking, shrugging off the pain, becoming tired, losing heart.

Wounds are a measure of how much punishment the target can take before its body gives up. Narrate Wound damage as (gruesome) physical injuries.

A Hunter or Entity with 0 Resolve is Broken. Often, Entities have an ability that triggers When First Broken. If the Entity recovers Resolve and becomes Broken again, this ability doesn’t trigger a second time. Successful attacks against a Broken target inflict the attack’s listed Wound damage instead of Resolve.

An Entity with 0 Wounds is dead. A Hunter with 0 Wounds is dying. If they have any Resolve remaining, they lose it when they are reduced to 0 Wounds. They must succeed on a Hard roll on their turn to revive themselves with 1 Resolve and 1 Wound; if they fail, they die and awaken back at the Sanctum. A Hunter can revive a dying Hunter to 1 Resolve and 1 Wound by making a Guard manoeuvre in their area and forgoing the usual Resolve recovery.

Threat

You have two main ways to add Threat to the grid:

  • When a Hunter hits with an attack, place 1 Threat on their area in response.
  • When the Entity’s turn ends, place Threat equal to the Entity’s Threat Per Round. After the Entity has made both of their actions, at the end of their turn, place Threat as follows:

Entities spend Threat in three ways:

  • After a Hunter’s turn ends, you can spend Threat to trigger an Interrupt action in the target area.
  • When an Entity makes an attack, you may spend Threat in their target area to increase all defence rolls made against that attack by 2 for each Threat spent. If the attack affects multiple areas, each of these areas is handled separately, and Threat spent in an area affects all defence rolls made in that area.
  • Some attacks or manoeuvres require Threat to be spent as part of their activation. Costs for individual abilities are listed in their descriptions.
